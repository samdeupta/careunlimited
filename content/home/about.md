Care Unlimited commenced operations in the year 2014. It felt the need for support to our older citizens. A section of our citizens that is often lonely as family and friends become fewer and distanced. Natural degeneration, less social interaction due to mobility issues, loss of confidence and social neglect prevents them from pursuing an active and fulfilling life.
