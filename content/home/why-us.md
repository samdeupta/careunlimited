At Care Unlimited, we understand the children’s concern for their elderly parents. We realize how helpless they feel when they are often unable to help during challenges that come their way. Care Unlimited provides a reliable support system to the elderly, taking on the role of a surrogate child who listens to them, helps them with their chores, engages with them and reduces their loneliness.

We help prepare an Emergency Plan containing contact details of the ‘Go-To’ persons or institutions during an emergency. This enables the household to be adequately prepared to handle an emergency. However, for those who desire more involved support during medical emergencies, our partnership with an expert organization specializing in providing emergency health care 24 × 7 × 365 would cover for this risk. To enable this facility, the Senior or his children need to enter into a tripartite agreement.
