### Neeta Wadekar
_Lives in Boston, USA, her 85 year old mother is availing our service for last five years_

![](imgs/t-neeta.jpg)

Surya and his team have done a wonderful job with their idea and vision of Care Unlimited. Indeed as their name suggests CU has helped me and my sisters take care of our 80 year old mother in all aspects of living alone in Kolkata, with a lot of ease. I recommend any one with older parents living in Kolkata to give Care Unlimited a try--you will not be disappointed.
