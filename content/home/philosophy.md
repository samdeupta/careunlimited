Aging stereotypes exist and we want to change that. Care Unlimited is emphatic in its vision to ‘change the experience of old age through active aging and meaningful engagement’. It has been clear right from the start that its services are for creating a difference in the lives of the elderly by building meaningful relationships with their clients, providing them with a reliable support system, become a surrogate family member who will listen to them, help them with their chores, engage with them and reduce their loneliness. Care Unlimited is inspired to *add life to their years instead of years to their lives.*
