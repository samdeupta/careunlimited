Care Unlimited commenced its operations in the year 2014. It saw the need for supporting our senior citizens. A section of the population that is often lonely because of family and friends becoming fewer and distanced. Natural degeneration, less social interaction due to mobility issues, loss of confidence and social neglect prevents them from pursuing an active and fulfilling life. It is accepted in society that old age results in these problems and so the general population ignores the pain and isolation of senior citizens, leaving them even more lonely. This is the time when they benefit from a strong support system, to help them overcome their disability and renew their passions and interests and feel confident.

## The Story Of Care Unlimited

Suryasish Gupta was a software engineer by profession. At the peak of his long and illustrious career as the practice head of an MNC, he decided to quit corporate life and answer to his heart’s calling — to help others. Today after 10+ years, Care Unlimited has persevered its vision and worked steadfastly to fulfill its objective of improving the lives of our seniors in our city. The message is loud and clear that positive mental state that includes a zest for life, ability to laugh and have fun, optimism, self-esteem is not just for the young but for all ages, especially our elderly citizens. And Care Unlimited is committed in being a vehicle for change to be able to improve the physical and mental wellbeing as well as the quality of life for the elder community. It is gratifying to watch the progress of our elderly extended family who has gained immense confidence, even participating in cultural programs, socialising, actively pursuing their talents and looking forward to another day with positivity. However, the road is long and wide and there is much more to be achieved.

As a part of its outreach activities, Care Unlimited has started extending its concept of holistic care to rural Bengal. Though the execution model is different in rural Bengal, we are already getting positive response from the 500+ seniors we are covering.

Some of the other innovative concepts anchored by Care Unlimited worth mentioning –

- **Tree planting by rural households to increase green cover** – they are given fruit tree saplings to plant in their backyard and nurture them to reach maturity and bear fruits.
- **Hands-on-science lessons for small children** – to arouse their interest in science through simple experiments.

Care Unlimited advocates innovation in the social space and strives to challenge ourselves towards the betterment of our seniors and our community.

## Our team is our pride

Our compassionate and dedicated staff strive to achieve a successful outcome for all our members. They aim to provide a professional, consistent and reliable service including competent crisis management. They become a part of the family and endeavour to provide personalised care at all times.

## Mission & Vision

![Mission & Vision](img/vision-msn.png)

### Build everlasting relationships with our Elderly Members.

Fixated views and prejudices regarding old age exist and we want to change that. Care Unlimited is emphatic in its vision to ‘change the experience of old age through active aging and meaningful engagement’. It has been clear right from the start that its services are for creating a difference in the lives of the elderly. It is to make meaningful relationships with their clients, providing them with a reliable support system, become a surrogate family member who will listen to them, help them with their chores, engage with them and reduce their loneliness.
