Our services are crafted to inspire our elders to engage with life purposefully which would lead to a positivity that is crucial to mental wellbeing. We thus arrange our services, outlined below, depending on the preferences of each individual:

1. **Hold intellectually stimulating discussions** – Understand the senior’s interest and facilitate discussions accordingly. e.g. with an ex-first-division football player, discuss footballers of the 60’s & 70’s when Bengal football was in its heydays.

2. **Give them a sense of achievement** – Rekindle their desires, make them believe that their wishes / dreams can still be achieved. e.g. facilitate restarting long lost hobbies like painting, singing; Get them activity oriented - motivate them to play games - make them feel positive when they win a game or solve a puzzle.

3. **Arrange for new social connections for them** – organize small size social gatherings where they feel at ease with others.

4. **Design positivity boosters** – encourage them to write articles/stories from their memories of the days gone by; collect those writings to publish an **annual magazine**. Create **thematic calendars** where the pages are filled with pictures of the activities of our elders, their photos.

5. **Understand & improve their positivity level** – Use our proprietary review framework (designed with consultations with academicians) to assess and plan improvements.

6. **Eradicate their worries** – by addressing their daily chores.

7. **24X7 Medical Emergency Coverage** – provided through a partnership with an organization specialized in the field of medical management.
